# NPC Style Update Notes

## What changed

- NPCs are now explicitly a mixed-fruit cast, not mango-only.
- The playable protagonist may remain mango.
- Headphones are no longer a default accessory.
- Clothing and accessories must better match ordinary Bangladesh social roles.
- The S03 NPC character sheet was regenerated to reflect these rules.

## Example approved roles

- Rickshaw driver
- Tea stall owner
- Shopkeeper
- Student
- CNG driver
- Office worker
- Elderly man
- Street vendor

## Preferred accessories

- cap
- topi
- gamcha
- scarf
- beard
- apron
- satchel bag
- safety vest
- sandals
- sunglasses or visor

## Limited-use accessories

- Headphones only for selected student or modern youth variants.
