## Attribution and permissions
OSM raw geographic data is open with conditions, but the project's public tile server is not an unlimited free game map API. Original or correctly licensed in-game art, photographs, voices and ambient recordings are required. Copyrighted signs/logos, tourist imagery and 3D building extractions are not automatically free merely because they are publicly visible.
