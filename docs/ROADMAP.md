# Roadmap: stages to a finished game

Work goes one stage at a time. Each stage ends with a report and the owner's go-ahead for the next. This list holds everything the owner has asked for, as well as what `Guide Docs` still requires.

## Stage 1: fix what feels wrong (done, version 0.3)

- Character flicker removed
- Vehicles sit level; realistic pitch and roll; steering calmer at speed
- Drivers' hands on the real wheel or handlebars, feet on the floor, on all nine vehicle types
- Solid carts, stalls and people; people walk both ways, keep left and step aside; a bump gets a reaction and a Bangla line
- Traffic stays off the footpath
- Overhead traffic signals with bigger lamps; 33 traffic police, most at the kerb in high-vis
- Clock runs through a full day and night, with a setting for its pace; brighter nights
- Big map on M, with world size, names, scale bar, zoom and pan
- Sliders for how many people and vehicles
- Only some people wear glasses; traditional dress and working roles have a plain fruit face

## Stage 2: interactions (done, version 0.4)

- Only male voices: the browser's male Bangla voice by name, or text alone if it has none
- A general interaction system (GDD): anything new registers what it offers, and the prompts and keys work the same
- A menu of choices, with lines shown above people's heads
- Talk to anyone: greet, ask what they do, a topic of their own, local news, their name, directions, goodbye
- Directions: they point, and a marker appears on the minimap, the big map and the screen
- Pulling doors open with the hand on the handle, and shutting them after; real doors on the CNG and bus
- Crash reactions: non-violent, the other vehicle rocks and is pushed, carts slide and get wheeled back, the other driver shouts or climbs out to have a word and can be apologised to, people turn to look, police whistle
- Tea stall: order, sit down, a timer while eating, talk to the person alongside or the owner, finish quicker, or leave
- NPCs stop to talk to each other and are overheard, go and look at stalls, and stall owners call out
- Inspect a place; wave down a CNG or rickshaw

## Stage 3: crowds, driving, voice and transport (done, version 0.5)

Asked for along the way:

- Far more people and vehicles, up to about 1,140 people and 270 vehicles around you with the sliders at full
- Character jitter removed: walking legs no longer jump when someone changes speed
- Steadier driving: the view is fixed to the vehicle, frame timing is smoothed, and steering and bike lean are softer
- Traffic: drivers each have a temper, some speed, they overtake and change lanes in a smooth curve, bikes thread through, and nobody blocks a junction
- Crashes: real knocks by weight, off-centre hits swing vehicles round, dust and sparks, people clipped by a vehicle hop aside

From the plan:

- Free AI Bangla male voices, generated offline with six voice types, played from the game's own files
- Volume sliders for voices, surroundings, vehicles and other sounds; mute; subtitles on or off
- Bus: the conductor asks where you're getting off, and the bus takes you there
- People visibly climbing on and off buses, one at a time, and conductors calling the route
- CNG and rickshaw: pick a destination, the driver quotes a fare, haggle, then be driven there by road

## Stage 4: Mirpur 10 finished, two more districts, travel (done, version 0.6)

Fixes the owner listed:

- Shop fronts are solid, and the hero no longer ends up squeezed inside vehicles
- The invisible block at Kazipara is gone: a metro pier stood in the junction, and market carts stood in the road
- Only buses, CNGs, rickshaws and yellow taxis take passengers, not private cars
- Q asks what to call. A rickshaw, CNG or taxi drives to you. A bus doesn't: its helper shouts whether there's room, and the nearest stop gets marked
- People away from their shops or vehicles have nothing to sell and say so
- A driver blocked by the hero or by a parked vehicle sounds the horn and shouts at about two seconds, then goes round
- Far more cars, bikes, rickshaws and bicycles at full traffic, up to about 330 near you. Jams clear by themselves rather than locking up

New:

- **Gulshan:** wide avenues, glass towers, walled houses, a lake. **Dhanmondi:** apartments, numbered roads, a lake, the metro on the east side
- **Travel:** drive out on the right road; hire a CNG or taxi; take one of the few buses that go there, as most stay local; or take the metro between Mirpur 10 and Shahbagh. Choose a district on the title screen. Money and purchases carry over
- **Map:** building outlines, lakes, flyover, metro, roads out with arrows, click to mark a place, zoom buttons and a small map of how the three districts connect
- **Footbridge and station:** walk up and across, with rails that hold you in and people using them too
- **Flyover:** on Mirpur Road. Traffic uses it and so can you, by car or on foot
- **Money and shops:** 5,000 taka to start, shopkeepers in the nearest shops, carts that sell things, and fares and tea that really cost something
- **Metro:** ticket counter, gates, platform, a train that eases in, and a ride to the other station
- **Playground:** boys playing cricket and football, by themselves, and you can kick the football
- **School and university** in each district
- **Outfits:** three per character, except police and hero, and people vary in height

Moved to later: rain and other weather, and rooftop detail.

## Stage A: a real city (done, version 0.7)

- Fixes: vehicles no longer go inside each other, the station footpath is clear, horn while driving, hands stay out of the body, more green at lights, the run cycle redone, people dodging a car each move differently
- Menu: fits any window from 640 by 420 up, tabs, more settings, three save slots, saves position and hour, carries on where you left off
- Physics: real weight on real tyres for whatever you drive, grip that depends on the road, handbrake slides, hits that bounce and spin both vehicles by weight and where they land, bikes that fall over, rider thrown clear unhurt and the bike left lying until picked up, bins, chairs, crates, cones and barriers that scatter
- Bigger districts: all three now about 1.7 km across, about 80 blocks each, more roads, several parks and ponds, a river along one edge with boats and an embankment
- Recognisable places. Mirpur: stadium, zoo, botanical garden, Turag, Shah Ali and Benarasi markets. Gulshan: glass towers, the diplomatic quarter with flags and guard posts, the lake, the Balu. Dhanmondi: the lake, Rabindra Sarobar's open-air stage, coaching-centre streets, New Market, Nilkhet, the Buriganga
- Local shops: tong tea, paan, fuchka, jhalmuri, pitha, green coconut, sugarcane juice, biryani, rice hotels, barbers, tailors, cobblers, mobile repair, photocopy and passport photos, mobile-money agents, diagnostic centres, fish, meat and vegetable markets with haggling, sari, electronics, gold and book markets, second-hand clothes on the footpath
- Crowding: hawkers shoulder to shoulder, people walking in the road, crossing anywhere, running for buses and hanging off the doors, hawkers working the jam at red lights, layers of boards and banners, dogs and crows, garment workers at shift change, students at closing time
- Phone (P): calls, messages, contacts, navigation with the way drawn on the map, mobile money with a PIN and agents, ride booking, a camera that notes what's in shot, a job inbox
- Vehicles: leguna, covered van, garbage truck, ambulance with lamps and siren that traffic makes way for, ride-share bikes, police car, wrecker
- People: women, a schoolgirl, a boy, an old woman, a doctor, a guard, a sweeper, a businessman
- Buildings you can enter: shopping mall, restaurants, hotel, clinic, bank, an apartment block with your home, an office tower, a police station, with stairs and lifts
- Police: a station in each district, patrol cars you can't get into, officers who get out and talk, fines, a wrecker for anything left in a lane, the yard where it ends up, a fines desk
- Getting around: metro between all three districts, more bus routes and stops, the helper calls out each stop
- Weather: rain with a frequency setting, umbrellas, raincoats, people running, slippery roads, waterlogged low streets, thunder
- City rhythm: rush hours, the five prayer times, shops shutting at night, load-shedding, fuel station queues, Eid
- Playable: five characters of different ages and backgrounds, with different money, pace and what they're allowed to do; the boy can't drive but plays cricket

Still needs a person: women and girls have text but no voice until the owner approves the download of a woman's voice model.

## Stage B: the game (built, version 0.8)

- Health, money and usable items: eat, read, medicine. A health bar with no wounds or blood; at zero you wake at a clinic and pay a fee
- Phone features tied into the story
- Branching conversations with memory, about 2,000 lines to begin with
- Friends
- A mission system
- A 20-mission story: uncle's tea stall and a scam ring, go there and do it, no fighting. Not copied from the owner's earlier awareness games
- Jobs
- Differences between the characters that matter in play
- Everyday scams
- Extras, tutorial, ending, free play

Not included, by the owner's word: browser testing and playtesters (later), logos, public hosting, more cities, English and touch controls.

## Stage 5: more of the country

- Cox's Bazar and Saint Martin's as the first places away from Dhaka, as the docs plan
- More Dhaka districts, built from the same system
- Weather, including rain with people taking out umbrellas
- More detail in each district: rooftops, landmarks of its own, and checking the layouts against the real places

## Stage 6: missions and cybersecurity

- Accepting missions and the full mission gameplay
- Shopkeepers and others teaching cybersecurity; story missions; scam-awareness interactions with choices

## Stage 7: release

- English option; touch controls and phones
- Chrome and Firefox testing, playtests with 5 to 10 people, native Bangla review of all text
- Public hosting, if wanted

## After Stage B

Built in the owner's order: health and items, first person; empty spaces filled; mission system and the 20 missions; conversations, friends, character differences; jobs and everyday scams; tutorial, ending and free play; sound, menu, English and the pushing fix; garage, licence, radio and extras.

Still needs a person: native Bangla review, listening to the voices, playing by hand with a real mouse, other browsers, and the decision on a woman's voice model.
